## 📊 1. Base Fluid Architecture (VG/PG Ratios)

The ratio between Vegetable Glycerin (VG) and Propylene Glycol (PG) dictates vapor density, throat hit, and overall coil wicking efficiency. Matching this ratio to your specific hardware setup is vital to avoid dry hits or leaking.

| VG/PG Ratio | Vapor Production | Throat Hit | Target Hardware System | Recommended E-Liquid Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **70% VG / 30% PG** | High / Dense | Smooth | Sub-Ohm Tanks / Rebuildables (RTA/RDA) | Freebase Shortfills (Large Formats) |
| **50% VG / 50% PG** | Moderate / Stealth | Medium | Pod Systems / High-Resistance Coils | Nicotine Salts / MTL E-liquids |
| **60% VG / 40% PG** | Balanced | Medium-Light | Versatile Pods (RDL) & Small Tanks | All-Purpose Freebase / Mid-Range Pods |

## ⚡ 3. Nicotine Salts (Nic Salts) vs. Freebase Formulations

Nicotine salts utilize an optimized organic chemical compound (typically salicylate or benzoate) to lower pH levels. This modification delivers an incredibly smooth experience even at highly concentrated levels (10mg/ml and 20mg/ml), making them fundamentally distinct from standard freebase nicotine.

*   **Freebase Nicotine:** High pH levels. Delivers a prominent throat hit. Slow absorption rate. Best suited for low-nicotine sub-ohm setups.
*   **Nicotine Salts (Nic Salts):** Low pH levels. Near-zero harshness. Fast bloodstream absorption (entering the system in under 7 seconds). Optimized exclusively for low-power pod systems operating at resistances $\ge 0.6\Omega$.

## 🛡️ Laboratory Safety Protocol for DIY Compounding

1. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Always handle high-concentration nicotine boosters using nitrile gloves and safety eyewear. Nicotine absorbs quickly through dermal contact.
2. **Aroma Concentration Limits:** Never vape raw flavor concentrates. Standard compounding safety limits require target dilution levels between **10% and 20%** inside the final volume, utilizing calibrated raw **[vaping base solutions](https://vapora.online/diy/bases/)**.
3. **Storage & Preservation:** To prevent premature oxidation of your nic shots, store finished blends in a dark, temperature-controlled environment, completely out of reach of children and pets.
